About Me

My interest in cybersecurity started young actually... watching action films where technology was the weapon, the key, and the solution. That curiosity led me to Computer Science in high school and eventually across the world to UWE Bristol, where I've spent three years turning that excitement into real skills.

Originally from Jakarta, Indonesia, studying abroad was the best decision I ever made. It taught me resilience, independence, and the courage to face discomfort — lessons no classroom could fully teach.

To me, cybersecurity is both protection and exploration — defending what matters, understanding how things break, and staying curious in a world that never stops changing.

I'm not just building a career. I'm building a life with no borders.

Hunter XP Case - Digital Forensic Investigation

Completed

Conducted a full-chain digital forensic investigation on a disk image using EnCase and Autopsy for dual-tool verification. Performed evidence acquisition and hash verification, system and registry analysis, internet history and email reconstruction, file carving, and password recovery. Produced court-standard documentation — contemporaneous notes, witness statement, and forensic report — mapping digital findings to relevant UK legislation.

Cybersecurity Consultancy — Industry Client: Leonardo

Completed

Delivered a consultancy presentation to an industry and academic panel, proposing a Security Culture framework for Leonardo built on NIST CSF, ISO 27001, CIS Controls, and GDPR. As part of a 5-person team, led the "Delivering Success" section — identifying key adoption challenges (employee resistance, budget constraints, leadership alignment) with strategic responses, and designing a success metrics framework spanning leading indicators (training completion, phishing simulation rate) and lagging indicators (incident volume, MTTD/MTTR).
